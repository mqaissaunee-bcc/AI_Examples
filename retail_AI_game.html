How scoring works
  • Your final score is the average of efficiency, customer satisfaction and security.
  • 85% or more is excellent, 70% is on track, 50% leaves room to improve.
  • Achievements: 90% efficiency, 95% satisfaction, 90% security, and five or more technologies.
  • The "expected return" shown when you buy a technology is a game estimate, not a real-world figure.
