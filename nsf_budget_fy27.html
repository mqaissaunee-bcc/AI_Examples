Context

A request, not a budget. The President proposes; Congress appropriates. Final funding is set through the appropriations process.
Last year's outcome. The FY 2026 request also proposed about $3.9 billion. Congress instead provided $8.75 billion in the appropriations act signed January 23, 2026.
Where FY 2027 stands. No FY 2027 appropriation for NSF has been enacted. The government is operating under a continuing resolution through December 11, 2026.
One directorate eliminated. The request provides no funding for the Social, Behavioral and Economic Sciences directorate. Only its statistics center (NCSES) would continue, at $44 million, down from $74 million.
Outside the headline figure. The request also includes $900 million in mandatory funding for an Antarctic research vessel, for $4.86 billion in total.

    Research capacity

    About one proposal in five was funded in FY 2025. At the requested level, fewer than one in twelve would be.

    People

    Roughly 211,000 fewer researchers, students, postdocs and teachers would take part in NSF-supported work.

    Education

    STEM Education would fall by about 64% from FY 2025, and its account would be merged into the research account.

    Priorities named in the request

    Artificial intelligence, quantum information science, advanced manufacturing, biotechnology and research infrastructure. NSF-wide AI and quantum funding would still fall by about a third.
