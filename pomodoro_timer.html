    How to use it

    1. Pick one task before you press Start.
    2. Work on only that task until the timer ends. If something else comes to mind, write it down for later.
    3. Take the short break away from your screen.
    4. After four focus blocks, take the long break.

    Press the space bar to start or pause. The timer keeps correct time if you switch tabs.
