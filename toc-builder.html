Contents Builder

Word can only build a clickable table of contents from text it recognises as a heading. This tool reads your manuscript, works out which lines are headings even when they were only ever made bold and bigger, and hands the decisions back to you before it changes anything.

Nothing leaves this page. Your document is opened in the browser. There is no upload and no server.

        Then, in Word

        1. Open the downloaded file. If Word asks to update fields, say yes. If the contents still says "Right-click here", right-click it and choose Update Field, then Update entire table.
        2. Check it. Ctrl-click (Windows) or Cmd-click (Mac) an entry to jump to that section.
        3. For PDF, use File › Save as and pick PDF. The headings become clickable PDF bookmarks automatically.
        4. For Pressbooks or LibreTexts, import the Word file directly. Both read heading styles to split the book into chapters.

        Real heading styles also give you Word's Navigation pane and let screen readers move through the book by section — which most OER accessibility reviews require.
