What changed in 2026

AI use is the norm

88% of organizations used AI in 2025, and generative AI reached 53% adoption within three years, faster than the personal computer or the internet. [30]

Capable, and still unreliable

Models now match or beat human performance on some PhD-level science questions, yet can falter at basic tasks. [30]

Jobs: an uneven picture

Projections to 2030 show 170 million roles created and 92 million displaced, while entry-level software jobs for ages 22 to 25 fell about 20% in a year. [29] [30]

Cheaper every year

The smallest models from the major providers are listed at about $0.10 per million input tokens. [31]

About this resource

The myths draw on peer-reviewed studies, industry reports and reference works published between 2018 and 2026. Every claim in a "reality" section carries a numbered citation; select one to jump to its source.

This page is a snapshot. AI changes quickly, so check the cited sources for anything you plan to rely on.
